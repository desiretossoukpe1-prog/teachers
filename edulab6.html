Who we are

Education solutions grounded in context.

EDULAB (Education Laboratory) is an education consulting and services initiative focused on helping people and institutions navigate educational challenges and opportunities. We believe effective support begins with listening, understanding local realities, and working collaboratively. Our work is guided by research and by a commitment to improving education across Africa.

Meet the founder

An educator answering a calling.

Fulbright ScholarMultiple competitive scholarshipsMA, Teaching English as a Second LanguagePhD candidate, Educational PsychologyUniversity of ArizonaNearly a decade in the classroomMentor to international students

Désiré Tossoukpe is an educator, researcher, and Fulbright Scholar, and the recipient of multiple competitive scholarships. After nearly a decade of teaching in middle and high schools, he moved to the United States on a Fulbright scholarship and earned an MA in Teaching English as a Second Language (TESL). He is now a third-year PhD student in Educational Psychology, with a minor in Higher Education, at the University of Arizona.

His research examines how program evaluation informs educational policy and how early academic specialization shapes identity and career choices. He also studies the factors that affect access and success for first-generation, low-income (FGLI) students, including sense of belonging and identity development. Drawing on data from across the African continent, he works to develop evidence-based solutions for education in Africa.

As a graduate student, Désiré invests in the African community and supports its members at every step, helping to build resilience, foster connection, and pay it forward across the African diaspora worldwide. He has also mentored many international students, helping them settle in the United States and giving them the procedural knowledge they need for a successful academic and professional journey. He has presented his work on education and evaluation at conferences, where he also learns from diverse voices in the field. Outside academia, he enjoys songwriting, travel, and geopolitics.

A calling, not just a career

I have seen what a committed teacher can do with very little, and what evidence can do when it is rooted in local reality. EDULAB is my answer to a calling: to use research, experience and community to help improve education across Africa, one learner, one teacher and one school at a time.
